Classical Countdown

An annual WETA Classical tradition. Listeners vote for their favorite classical music pieces and we play back the top 100 pieces during Thanksgiving week.

A Thanksgiving Tradition on WETA Classical

WETA Classical's annual listeners' choice competition lets YOU determine the most popular classical pieces in the DC area. The power is in your hands to curate an exciting Classical Countdown that represents your favorite works. Here's to an exciting race this year, and be sure to tune in Thanksgiving week to hear the results!

Scroll through our list of choices below and pick your top three, whether they be Mozart, Bach, Beethoven or something a bit more obscure. Who will emerge triumphant? Will we see another upset of perennial champion Beethoven's Ninth? It's up to you to decide.
